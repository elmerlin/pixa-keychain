# Code of Conduct

Be kind, and assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination aren't welcome here, and neither is
pressuring anyone to share a key or send funds.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Maintainers may
remove comments, code or people that break it.

To report something, message `@pixtopia` on Pixa with an encrypted memo, or contact the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/elmerlin).
